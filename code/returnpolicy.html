Return Policy

Simple returns for gear that does not work out.

Last updated: September 2026

7-day returns

You can return most items within 7 days of delivery if they are unused, in original packaging, and have all tags and accessories.

What we cannot take back

  • Used or washed items, including gloves, helmets, and jerseys
  • Shuttlecocks and balls once the packaging is opened
  • Items damaged after delivery through misuse
  • Customised or personalised products

Damaged or wrong items

If your item arrives damaged or is not what you ordered, contact us within 48 hours with your tracking ID and a photo. We will send a replacement or refund you in full, including delivery.

How to return

  • Contact support from the Help Center with your tracking ID and the reason.
  • We confirm your request within one working day and arrange pickup or share a return address.
  • Pack the item securely in its original packaging.

Refunds

After we inspect the item, refunds are sent within 5–7 working days. Mobile banking payments are refunded to the same bKash or Nagad number, card payments to the original card, and Cash on Delivery orders by bKash or Nagad. The delivery fee is refunded only for damaged or wrong items.

Exchanges

Need a different size or model? Request an exchange within 7 days. It depends on stock availability.
